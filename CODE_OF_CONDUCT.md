# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## What that looks like

Behaviour that keeps this a place people want to contribute to:

- Assuming good faith, and asking before concluding someone acted badly.
- Giving and receiving review comments about the work rather than the person.
- Saying "I was wrong" without ceremony when you were.
- Remembering that a question you find obvious was not obvious to you once.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are
handled privately, and reporters' identities are not disclosed.

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and
will explain their reasoning when doing so.

## Enforcement

Responses are proportionate and, in most cases, start with a private
conversation.

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the problem, for a first or minor
   incident.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation or repeated behaviour after a
   warning.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a sustained pattern, harassment, or aggression toward
   individuals or groups.
